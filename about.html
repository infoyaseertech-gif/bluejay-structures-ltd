About Bluejay

A registered, multi-sector contractor built for accountability.

Incorporated in 2023, Bluejay Structures Ltd operates across construction, mobility, food supply and government procurement — governed by one set of professional and regulatory standards.

Executive summary

Structured for delivery, not just for growth.

Bluejay Structures Ltd is a dynamic and fast-growing Nigerian company committed to excellence in service delivery across the construction and infrastructure sector. Incorporated on 4th September 2023 under the Companies and Allied Matters Act (CAMA 2020), the company has rapidly established itself as a trusted name in the delivery of high-quality construction projects, real estate development, and supply of materials.

With a strong operational presence in Katsina State and a growing reach across Nigeria, Bluejay Structures Ltd specialises in Building Construction, Road Construction, Project & Property Management, Distribution and Supply of Dangote Cement, and Automobile Sales & Hire. Our core philosophy is anchored in integrity, innovation, and client-centered delivery.

The company is positioned as a trusted partner for government agencies, private developers, and corporate clients — delivering cost-effective, technically sound, and aesthetically superior infrastructure. Through a team of registered engineers, architects, and construction managers, Bluejay Structures Ltd has the professional capacity to handle projects of varying scale and complexity.
